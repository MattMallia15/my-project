# Current Market View — 2026-06-17

## Top-line
**Significantly overvalued.** The tape is not pricing in any negative outlook; it's acting as if AI alone keeps everything rising. "If one piece of the jigsaw drops, the whole thing falls." Long-term I am worried.

## AI thesis
- I believe in AI structurally the way I would have believed in the internet.
- But this is shaped like the internet bubble: a few real winners, lots of losers.
- Near-term hype expected to **intensify** over the next ~3 months as Anthropic, OpenAI, and SpaceX IPO chatter builds. Retail flows will chase.
- Tactical AI riders in the book: **PLTR, APH, MU**. These are short-lived, NOT value holdings.

## Risk window
- **Sept-Nov 2026.** One scare in this window could trigger the correction.
- **Q3 prints are the moment of truth.** Q2 weakness will be excused as Strait-of-Hormuz fallout. If the promised post-reopening rebound doesn't show up in Q3, the market panics.
- Sitting through Q2; tape won't react until Q3.

## Plan
1. **Now → ~3 months:** Hold the tactical AI sleeve. Don't add aggressively. Trim into strength on names that have run (e.g., ACN @ $215, WBD @ $29).
2. **~3-4 months out:** Reshape the book — rotate out of hype, build the undervalued sleeve, identify AI losers and broader laggards trading cheap.
3. **Through the correction:** Use built-up cash + monthly DCA to add value names aggressively.

## What would change my view
- Q2/Q3 prints come in materially stronger than expected → re-evaluate the bubble call.
- Fed shifts dovishly into the risk window → correction may be deferred; I should re-think timing.
- Macro shock outside AI (rates spike, credit event, geopolitical escalation) → correction may come sooner.
- AI capex narrative cracks (hyperscaler guidance cuts) → pull tactical sleeve faster.
